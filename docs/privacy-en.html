Privacy Policy — Agios Nektarios

Last updated: 19 August 2026

Agios Nektarios is an app for the residents of the settlement of Agios Nektarios, Attica, Greece. It is not a commercial app. It carries no advertising and sells nothing.

Who holds the data

Data is held in Firebase (Google Firestore, Google Authentication, Google Cloud Messaging), operated by Google Ireland Limited. The data controller is the administrator of the village app; contact details at the end.

What is collected, and why

Data Why Who can see it
First and last name So neighbours know who wrote what All signed-in residents
Email address To sign in The resident, and the administrator
Telephone number Only if you enter one You and the administrator only — see below
Profile photo Optional All signed-in residents
Location of a report or alarm To place it on the map All signed-in residents
Location of your own house So an ambulance can be told where to come You alone. Not even the administrator
Photographs attached to reports To show the problem All signed-in residents
Messages and comments They are the content of the app The people in that conversation
Device push token To deliver notifications No resident

The app does not collect advertising identifiers, browsing history, your phone's contacts, or any financial or health data.

Your telephone number

Your number is stored privately. No other resident can read it — this is enforced by the server's security rules, not by the app.

There is one exception and it is yours to make: if you turn on "Let neighbours text me in an emergency", your number becomes readable by other residents' phones so that the alarm screen can text everyone at once. You can withdraw that at any time from Settings, and it is deleted immediately. An administrator can also switch the feature off for the whole village.

Text messages are sent by your own messaging app, from your own phone. This app only opens it with the message written; it does not send anything itself and cannot see what you sent.

Location

Location is requested only when you tap something that needs it — to put a report or an alarm at the right place on the map. The app does not record your location in the background and does not track you.

Weather

Forecasts and the fire-risk reading come from Open-Meteo. The app sends the fixed coordinates of the village, never yours, and no identifying information.

Maps

Map tiles come from OpenFreeMap, which requires no account. Your device's IP address reaches that provider, as it does with any internet request.

How long it is kept

For as long as your account exists. Alarms expire on their own (six hours for emergencies, seven days for outages). Reports and announcements remain, because they are the village's record.

Your rights

From inside the app you can view and correct your details, remove your house from the map, withdraw consent for texting, and delete your account (Settings → Account). Deleting removes your profile, your private documents and your telephone number. Reports and comments you wrote remain, because they belong to a shared conversation; ask the administrator if you want those removed too.

You also have the GDPR rights of access, rectification, erasure, portability and objection. Write to the address below.

Children

The app is for adult residents. It is not designed for children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16.

Changes

If anything material changes, the date at the top changes and the new version is published here.
